# P5 — Scroll pad

## Already built

- Done on 2026-10-17 (v0.3): the two-speed scroll pad with Stop, aimed at the pointer's last point outside the keyboard and outlined. It glides by wheel steps, falls back to `ScrollPattern`, and stops at the end, at a press, at the limit or when the target goes; it pages our open list, and a lone second click on Win opens Start ([plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-16-phase-5-scroll-pad.md), [test report](../test-reports/2026-10-17-p5-scroll-pad.md), ADR-0029).

## Notes

- None open. Findings for later items are in [P4](P4.md) and [P13](P13.md); the manual test's drag-off and no-uiAccess checks are in the test report (check 22, note 10).

## Ideas

- 2026-10-17 (owner, manual test): aim where the pointer last rested before the keyboard, not at its last point outside it. A small window's path to the keyboard crosses the big window behind it, which the pad then picks; a pause of about a third of a second (a setting) in the pointer's kept history would mark the window meant. The owner kept the wheel-like rule for P5.
- 2026-10-17 (G6 run 1, deferred): an end tolerance in pixels, so Explorer's bottom (99.991 %) reads as the end at once instead of after the wheel trips and the steps judge it.
