# P10 — Selection helper

## Already built

- Built (2026-11-09, v0.6): the selection strip with Copy, Cut, Paste and Translate, and Paste at a caret (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-selection-chips-design.md`, [test report](../test-reports/2026-11-08-p10-selection-chips.md)).

## Notes

- As designed, for the next reader:
  - VS Code's terminal and other apps that do not tell their selection show no strip, as the look reads only what UI Automation reports.
  - After a walk up from a focus with no text to the text above it, the strip offers Copy and Translate only, as the keys go to the focused element.
