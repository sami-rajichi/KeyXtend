## Findings

- **F1 Crop:** fixed by the frame with handles and Apply/Cancel (handfeel tasks 4 and 5).
- **F2 Darker editor:** fixed; each window sets its flags after its shadow's (task 1).
- **F3 Maximise:** fixed; `[snip] editor_full` (task 2).
- **F4 Delays:** fixed; the shadow waits for its window's first frame and the editor is made once (task 3).
- **F5 Video editor:** removed; the strip plays and speeds videos (task 6, ADR-0060).
- **F6 Countdown after a Cancel:** fixed; `[ui] frame_latency` defaults to 0 (task 10, note 3).
- **F7 Play's speed:** fixed; Play opens the copy at the picked speed, with a waiting line (task 9).
- **F8 Darker panel and close question:** fixed; both are made at start with their shadow first (task 9).
- **F9 A video pasted as its path:** accepted. Copy puts the video on the clipboard only as a file (a file list with a copy drop effect, no text), so a plain text field turns that file into its path; the spec now says so.
- **The shipped `frame_latency = 0`:** installed at 9a36156d, it passed G24, G8's key light and the final check (gate rows 8–10, note 4).
- **Save a video at 2×:** G24 clicks no video Save, so the final check ran it by hand and it passed (final row 2).

## Notes

1. Each snip's new frozen screens stacked above our windows; ADR-0059 now keeps them under our windows, and Esc ends a frozen snip.
2. The gate compared the worker's folder as text, so it missed the worker; fixed in 51474dab.
3. Qt's Direct3D frame-latency wait stalled a window shown again for about 1 s per frame; with `frame_latency = 0` every countdown second drew on time.
4. G8 ran from a signed `kx-gates` beside the keyboard, as unsigned clicks never reach a uiAccess window; its numbers match ADR-0025's, so Direct3D's own frame queue did not slow the key light.
