# Phase 11, part 1: voice typing on this PC (v0.7)

## Goal
- One click on the Dictation key starts listening in the keyboard's language, a bar shows the live wave, and Stop types the text. Ten local models run on the processor or a graphics card, with Compare models, a history and optional recordings.
- Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-voice-typing-design.md` (approved 2026-11-10; replaces spec §8.2 for the PC); mock-up `design/voice-bar.html`; `docs/FEATURES.md` P11; `docs/items/P11.md`; the move map's P11 rows. Part 2, the cloud services, is `2026-11-10-phase-11-voice-cloud.md`.

## Scope
- In: ADR-0042 to 0045; the `Speech` and `ModelFiles` ports, their hosts and fakes; the worker's streaming session; the `kx-ipc` voice and fetch frames; the worker's `voice` and `fetch` jobs with the `transcribe.cpp` engine; new `kx-platform-net` and `kx-mod-voice`; the keyboard's `voice` role and text route; the bar, the Voice panel, tokens, icons, sounds and texts; `[voice]` settings; G22 on the product; the spike's local voice parts removed.
- Out: cloud services (part 2); exporting recordings (F11); voice commands (F2); translation and read aloud (F8); the Settings window's page (P13); macOS and Linux adapters (P15).

## Design notes
- **Crates:** new `kx-mod-voice`, `kx-platform-net`; touched: `kx-module-api`, `kx-ipc`, `kx-platform`, `kx-platform-windows`, `kx-platform-fake`, `kx-mod-keyboard`, `kx-ui`, `kx-theme`, `apps/keyxtend`, `apps/keyxtend-worker`, `xtask`, `tools/kx-gates`.
- **ADRs:** 0042 (two worker jobs over a streaming session, one model per job, the sound hand-over, capability `Voice`); 0043 (one engine runner, the models table, defaults per language, the models folder and downloads; replaces ADR-0007's engine lines); 0044 (the engine's loadable parts, the card's checks, Automatic); 0045 (the bar takes clicks; replaces ADR-0013's caption line).
- **Capabilities:** new `Voice`, an event gate held by the module, the keyboard and the ui. The module also holds `Microphone` (speech), `Network` (model downloads only), `SecretStore` (the sealed shelf), `ReadFocusedText` (`FieldWatch`) and `WriteClipboard` (Copy).
- **Port `ports/speech.rs`** (`SpeechKey`, gated `Microphone`): `Speech::open(SpeechAsk)` gives a `SpeechRun` (`stop`, `cancel`, `again`, `keep`, `close`) and tells `SpeechNote`s on any thread: a level, heard or quiet, a piece's text, done with its times and memory, loading, warming, a fault. `Speech` also lists `Device`s (kind, name, total and free memory, driver), reads a file's `ModelFacts`, and runs a card's `check`. Text and sound are `Redacted`.
- **Port `ports/models.rs`** (`ModelFilesKey`, gated `Network`): `ModelFiles` scans a folder, fingerprints a file, fetches a row into a `.part` file (`FetchRun`: progress, pause, cancel), removes a file and tells the drive's room.
- **Events `ports/voice.rs`** (gated `Voice`): `VoiceKey` (keyboard: the key was clicked), `VoiceLit { rec }` (lights the key), `VoiceOpen` (tray or gear: open the panel), `VoiceView` (module to ui: the bar's state and the panel's page), `VoiceAct` (ui clicks, with the view's round), `TextAsk { round, erase, text, gap_ms }` and `TextDone { round, count, ok }` (the keyboard stays the only typist). Ungated: `VoiceLevel`, which holds no content.
- **Host:** `kx-platform/src/worker/stream.rs` is a session kind beside `Host::call`: asks with deadlines, plus notes the worker sends on its own. `kx-platform/src/speech/` and `models/` are the OS-neutral services over it: job life, `idle_min`, one model per job, the sound hand-over in wiped buffers.
- **Frames:** `kx-ipc/src/{voice,fetch}*.rs`, `Kind` codes from 19, limits in `limits.rs` (a text, a sound piece, a path, an address). The worker holds no table: every ask carries its fields.
- **Worker:** `Job::Voice` (microphone through `cpal`, resample, detector, cutter, trait `Engine` with the `transcribe.cpp` adapter, cards, the self-test; no network) and `Job::Fetch` (HTTPS through `kx-platform-net`, range resume, SHA-256; no microphone). It loads its parts from its own folder only. Cargo features: `engine` (on to start; CI's Linux lint runs without it), `card` (Vulkan; on in staged builds), `gate` (a sound file in place of the microphone; never staged).
- **Shipped files:** the detector model and three self-test clips (English, French, Arabic) under `assets/voice/`; the engine's loadable parts beside the worker.
- **Module tables:** `models.toml` (the ten rows with their addresses and allowed hosts, the order per language and the general order, each with its test date) and `score.toml` (scoring rules per language). Compare's set text is a translation: the ui hands the shown text to the module with the act, so it is written once.
- **Store:** `<data>/voice/` (`VOICE_DIR`), sealed with its own key: the history, the kept recordings in pieces, the counts, the cards' check results and the compare numbers.
- **Settings `[voice]`:** `on` true; `models_dir` ""; `picks` {}; `device` "auto" ("auto", "cpu" or a card's id); `threads` 0 (0–64); `idle_min` 5 (1–120); `record_max_s` 600 (10–3600); `piece_s` 30 (10–120); `pad_ms` 300 (0–2000); `nothing_s` 5 (2–60); `silence_stop` false; `silence_stop_s` 3 (1–30); `debounce_ms` 400 (100–2000); `type_suffix` " " (≤ 8 characters); `type_gap_ms` 10 (0–200); `done_ms` 9000 (2000–60000); `wave_ms` 300 (0–2000); `level_hz` 30 (5–60); `place` "above" ("above", "bottom" or "top"); `sounds` true; `history` true; `history_max` 50 (1–500); `keep_voice` false; `voice_max_mb` 1024 (64–65536); `card_margin_mb` 512 (0–8192); `card_gain` 1.5 (1–10); `card_diff` 1 (0–5); `min_free_mb` 2048 (256–65536); worker times as `[clipboard]`'s: `ask_ms` 3000, `load_s` 60, `warm_s` 180, `piece_wait_s` 120, `stall_s` 30.
- **Tokens:** `shape.toml [caption]` cut again to the mock-up (the bar and its buttons tied to the key size, the wave's bars, the language chip) and new `[voice]` (panel rows, marks, the progress line, the compare table); `motion.toml` keeps `caption`, `shimmer` and `dot`, and gains `wave` and `voice_page`; colours reuse `rec`, `rec_ink`, `rec_dot` and `pop_*`; `icons.toml [voice]` (bundled: mic, audio-lines, captions, copy, undo-2, x, settings-2, square, history, trash-2, pin, check, folder-open; new from Lucide: download, pause, play, cpu, a card icon, mic-off, rotate-ccw, scale); sounds `voice-start` and `voice-stop` from `cargo xtask sounds`.
- **Texts (EN, FR, AR), `i18n/voice/`:** `key.mic` and `label.mic` both say Dictation · Dictée · إملاء; `tray.voice`; `voice.{listening,transcribing,nothing,stop,cancel,close,copy,undo,again,anyway}`; `voice.note-*` (no microphone, in use, refused, unplugged, no model, unknown language, no fit, worker fault, pieces saved, too long, password); `voice.{models,device,compare,history,options}` and each page's words; `voice.mark-{default,ready,download,not-here,other}`; `voice.best`; `voice.untested`; `voice.read-text`; `module.voice`.

## Edge cases
1. Two clicks within `debounce_ms` count as one.
2. The language is fixed at Start; a layout change while listening does not change it.
3. Stop with no speech types nothing; after `nothing_s` the bar hints and keeps listening.
4. `record_max_s` stops and transcribes with a note; `silence_stop` stops after `silence_stop_s`, and is off to start.
5. Cancel types nothing and offers "Transcribe anyway" until the bar closes; Cancel while transcribing drops the run.
6. A password box: the text is typed, the bar shows dots and no Copy, nothing is kept.
7. Nothing in front takes text: it stays on the bar with Copy and in History; a box the system cannot read is typed into.
8. Undo insert erases exactly the text while nothing else was typed; after a key or a click it is dimmed.
9. Try again replaces the first text while Undo is possible; else the new text stays on the bar; History keeps the newest only.
10. The bar closes by Close, after `done_ms` or at a new dictation, and the sound is wiped each time; the pointer or an open panel keeps it open.
11. The job ended by `idle_min` while its bar is open: Try again is dimmed.
12. No microphone; in use; refused by the system; unplugged while listening: each has its note, and the key's light goes off.
13. The worker dies while listening or transcribing: a note, the pieces already told offered with Copy, never a hang.
14. Quit, lock or sleep while listening: stop, wipe, light off.
15. The keyboard hides while listening: the bar stays and Stop still works.
16. A hidden run: dictation works and nothing is kept.
17. No model yet: a note with Download. The file vanished: a note, and the row goes back to Download.
18. The model does not know the language: a note with the gear. No listed model knows it: the bar says so.
19. A pick naming a row no longer in the table falls back to the default, with the settings notice.
20. A bad fingerprint deletes the part; a cut, paused, resumed or cancelled download leaves no named file.
21. A full or missing drive, a read-only folder, or `min_free_mb` not met: no download, a note.
22. A redirect to an unlisted host, or an address that is not HTTPS, is refused.
23. A model too big for the PC is "Not for this PC" with the reason; with none usable the panel says so.
24. A card with too little free memory now is refused before loading, and the processor serves.
25. A card that reports an error mid-run: the same recording runs on the processor, and its check result is dropped.
26. The first warm-up runs in the background with progress; a driver update or a new model file runs the check again.
27. The self-test fails on text (`card_diff`) or on speed (`card_gain`): the card is not used and says why.
28. The card part is missing from the folder: the worker starts and no card is offered.
29. On a processor without AVX only the plainest processor part loads, and a dictation still works.
30. `threads` 0 means the real cores; a value above them is capped.
31. A 10-minute recording crosses the pipe and is kept in pieces under the limits.
32. History at `history_max` drops the oldest unpinned; `voice_max_mb` drops the oldest unpinned recording; Delete all asks first.
33. Arabic text on the bar reads right to left with the buttons' order kept; a long text shows its first lines.
34. A text longer than `text_cap` is typed in pieces, and its Undo erases in pieces.
35. A bad `place` or `device` in the file goes back to its default, with the settings notice.
36. The module off: no bar, no key light, and the worker never starts.

## Security
- Capabilities: `Voice` (event gate), `Microphone`, `Network` (downloads only), `SecretStore`, `ReadFocusedText`, `WriteClipboard`; no `InjectInput`, the keyboard stays the only typist.
- Data: sound (secret; worker memory, wiped at the bar's close; sealed only with `keep_voice`); text (`Redacted`; sealed history); model files (untrusted data, read only in the sandboxed job); addresses and fingerprints (public, from the table); counts (numbers only).
- Threats: a hostile model file; a forged or oversized frame or note; a download swapped or redirected; a planted library beside a model (parts load from the worker's folder only); a path trick in `models_dir`; the microphone open with no bar or light; text or sound in a log; a password's text kept or copied; focus taken from the app; the `voice` job reaching the network.

- Device: check the card, dictate on it, then on Automatic and on the processor.

- The signed uiAccess build: the bar stays above the Start menu and never takes focus; Narrator reads each button's name.
- After `idle_min` the worker is gone from the task list; the system's microphone sign is off whenever no bar shows.
