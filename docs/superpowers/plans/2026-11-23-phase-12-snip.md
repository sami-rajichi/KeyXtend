# Phase 12: Snip and screen recording (v0.8)

## Goal
- One click on Snip takes a picture of a region, a window or the screen, or records a part or the screen; a keycap strip then copies, edits or saves it, and nothing is kept unless asked.
- Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-snip-design.md` (owner, 2026-11-23; mock-up `design/snip.html`); `docs/items/P12.md`. Builds on P11 part 2, which must be merged first.

## Scope
- In: the capture bar (bar B), the frozen screen, Region, Window, Full screen, Video region and Video full screen; the strip; countdown, recording with Mic and Sound, the red edge and the record bar; the editor (eleven tools, undo, redo, speed for videos); the close question; the system's Save and folder dialogs; the Snip panel and its tray item; the keyboard's Bubble, Hidden and Shown; copies kept by the clipboard history; G24 on the product; the spike's snip parts removed.
- Out (owner, 2026-11-23): OCR; pin on screen; scrolling capture; a timer for pictures; drawing on, trimming or pausing a video; GIF; auto-save; macOS and Linux adapters (P15).

## Design notes
- **New crates:** `kx-mod-snip` (the module, via `kx-new-module`); `kx-platform-record`, worker-only like `kx-platform-net`: the screen recorder, the speed change and the system's Save and folder dialogs (Windows: the capture API Task 1 picks, WASAPI microphone and loopback, Media Foundation H.264 and AAC into MP4, `IFileSaveDialog`). Tidy D4 keeps it out of `keyxtend`'s tree.
- **Touched:** `kx-module-api`, `kx-ipc`, `kx-platform`, `kx-platform-windows`, `kx-platform-fake`, `kx-mod-keyboard`, `kx-ui`, `kx-theme`, `apps/keyxtend`, `apps/keyxtend-worker`, `tools/kx-gates`, `xtask`, `spike/`.
- **Ports (`kx-module-api/src/ports/snip.rs`, `snip/`):**
  - `ScreenFreeze`: freezes every screen as raw pixels with the visible windows' frames, refused past `shot_cap_mb`.
  - `Recorder`: start a part at `fps` with its sound switches into a file, switch Mic or Sound, Stop, Cancel; tells the time, the end with the length, or a fault; `speed` writes a re-timed copy.
  - `FileAsk`: the system's Save dialog (folder, proposed name, PNG or MP4) and folder dialog; answers a path or nothing.
  - `MediaOpen`: plays a video of the Snips folder in the user's own player, through Explorer as `Reveal` does.
  - `ClipboardWriter::write_kept`: a write without our "leave out of history" markers, so both clipboard histories keep a snip.
  - `WindowGuard::shield` (`kx-platform` ui port): keeps one of our windows out of every capture (Windows: `WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE`).
- **Events (gated `ScreenCapture`):** `SnipStart` (the Snip key), `SnipView(Arc<SnipPage>)` (what each window draws), `SnipAct` (every click, corner and gesture point, the editor's flattened pixels), `SnipAside` (Bubble, Hidden or back), `SnipLit` (the key's light), `SnipLine` and `SnipLineKey` (the editor's text line, ADR-0040).
- **Capabilities of `kx-mod-snip`:** `ScreenCapture`, `Microphone` (Mic), `WriteClipboard`, `OpenFolder` (Open folder, Play), and a new `SaveFile` for `FileAsk`.
- **Worker jobs:** `record` (recorder and speed, normal rights, its own memory cap `record_mb`) and `pick` (the dialogs), each with the UI limits Task 1 proves; the `media` job gains `Encode` (raw pixels to PNG). The app builds a picture's `CF_DIB` from its own pixels and never decodes one.
- **Rendering:** Rust decides; QML draws the frozen screen (`image://snip`), the drawings (Qt Shapes and Text, so Arabic shapes right) and, for Copy and Save, flattens the editor's picture at the screen's pixel size into an act.
- **Folders:** `SNIP_DIR` "snips" in the data folder, with `unsaved/`; made at boot (`apps/keyxtend/src/snip.rs`). A hidden run (ADR-0037) makes none and dims Video and Save.
- **Settings `[snip]`:** `keyboard` "bubble"; `save_dir` ""; `countdown_s` 3 (0–10); `fps` 30 (15, 30); `max_min` 30 (1–120); `mic_on` false; `sound_on` false; `copied_days` 1 (1–7); `min_free_mb` 2048 (512–20480); `undo_max` 50 (10–200); `shot_cap_mb` 256 (64–1024); `record_mb` 512 (256–2048); `editor` {} (its last size and place).
- **Tokens:** `icons.toml [snip]` (Lucide: square-dashed, app-window, monitor, video, monitor-play, square-pen, circle-dot, volume-2, volume-x, crop, pencil, highlighter, circle, move-up-right, slash, type, list-ordered, maximize-2; the rest bundled); `shape.toml [snip]` grows the bar, strip, record bar, countdown, editor size and minimum, thicknesses and text sizes; `themes/common.toml` gains `snip_rec` and six `snip_ink_*` with the highlighter's alpha.
- **Texts (EN, FR, AR), `i18n/snip/`:** `snip.way-*`, `snip.hint-*`, `snip.strip-*`, `snip.done-*`, `snip.rec-*`, `snip.count`, `snip.tool-*`, `snip.ink-*`, `snip.ask-*`, `snip.panel-*`, `snip.note-*` (too big, disk low, longest reached, no video support, recording failed, save failed, clipboard busy), `tray.snip`.

## Edge cases
1. Snip key, Esc or Close while picking: nothing kept, the keyboard back as it was.
2. The same spot twice drops the corner; a part across two screens of different scaling keeps exact pixels.
3. Window: our own windows, cloaked and minimised windows are never offered.
4. A frozen copy past `shot_cap_mb`: a note, nothing frozen.
5. Copy with the clipboard busy: tries again, then a note; the strip stays.
6. Save: the dialog cancelled comes back to the strip; a full or read-only place gives a note.
7. Close asks only when not kept since the last change; Don't save deletes; Back and Esc return.
8. Record with less than `min_free_mb` free: Record dims with a note; mid-recording it stops and keeps the video.
9. `max_min` reached: stops, keeps the video, says so.
10. Cancel during the countdown: no file is made.
11. No microphone, or one refused: Mic dims with a tip; no sound device: Sound dims.
12. Mic or Sound switched mid-recording: silence while off, sound stays in step with the picture.
13. The worker dies mid-recording: a note, the broken file is deleted.
14. No Media Foundation (Windows N): both video ways dim with a tip.
15. A copied recording stays `copied_days`, then the start sweeps it; any other leftover in `unsaved/` goes at the next start.
16. Bubble: a click on it mid-recording brings the keyboard back, still out of the video; Hidden shows nothing of ours but the capture UI; Shown is in the picture.
17. The keyboard comes back after every end: Copy, Save, Close, Cancel, a fault.
18. Editor: Undo after Crop; the Eraser on an empty spot does nothing; `undo_max` drops the oldest step; Steps after Undo; a drawing begun outside the picture is clipped.
19. Editor: smaller than its minimum stops there; maximise and back; its size and place come back next time.
20. Speed 4× of a very short video still gives a playable file.
21. A hidden run: Video and Save dim; Copy and Edit work.
22. The Snip key while the editor holds unkept work asks first, then starts.
23. High contrast and reduced motion: system colours; the dot stops pulsing.
24. Arabic text in the editor, and Arabic, French and English everywhere in the snip's windows.
25. Frozen screens (ADR-0059): the bar, its hint, the strip, the panel and the keyboard stay above them at each stage and after a move or a new scale; an Esc from the hand or another program ends a pick or closes the strip, a second at the close question means Don't save, and during a countdown, a recording or the editor it stays the app's.

## Security
- Capabilities: `ScreenCapture`, `Microphone`, `WriteClipboard`, `OpenFolder` and the new `SaveFile`, held by `kx-mod-snip` alone.
- Data: screen pixels and recordings (private: memory, the clipboard, the user's chosen files and `unsaved/` only; never logged); sound (only while its switch is on); dialog answers (untrusted paths, checked).
- Threats: a secret on screen kept without the user's choice; our UI or the keyboard leaking into a capture; a path from the dialog outside a drive or with another type; shell extensions or codecs loading into the uiAccess process; the worker reaching the network; leftovers in `unsaved/`; sound heard while off.

## Manual Windows check
- Install the merged build. Snip: Region, Window, Full screen; Copy then paste into Paint and a chat; the clipboard history shows the picture.
- Edit: each tool once, Arabic text, Undo, Redo, Crop; Copy and Save; the editor's size comes back.
- Save opens Windows' dialog at the Snips folder; Change and Open folder work.
- Video region with Mic on, then Video full screen with Sound on: count, record 20 s, Stop; Copy into a chat and Explorer; Play; Speed 2×.
- Bubble, Hidden and Shown each once: the keyboard is in the picture only with Shown.
- Close without keeping asks; Don't save leaves nothing in `unsaved/`.
